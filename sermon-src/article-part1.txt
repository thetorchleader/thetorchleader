YORK COUNTY, Pennsylvania — Former pastor Kenneth "Kenny" Baldwin did not appear in court on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, after his defense attorney waived his formal arraignment in the York County Court of Common Pleas, according to the official court docket in Commonwealth of Pennsylvania v. Kenneth Kevin Baldwin, docket number CP-67-CR-0004840-2026.

The arraignment had been scheduled for 8:45 AM in Courtroom 7004 before Judge Clyde W. Vedder. Instead, on October 30, 2026, Baldwin's attorney, Peter McCrary Vaughn of Gettle Vaughn Law LLC in York, filed a "Waiver of Appearance at Arraignment." That same day, the York County District Attorney's Office filed the Information — the formal charging document in Common Pleas court — marked "Arraignment Waived." The November 4 hearing is now listed on the docket as "Cancelled," and the disposition reads "Proceed to Court (Arraignment Waived)," with the notation "Defendant Was Not Present."

No plea is recorded on the docket, and no trial date has been set. The case status is "Active," with a processing status of "Awaiting Trial." Baldwin remains free on $80,000 unsecured bail, posted on July 22, 2026.

Baldwin, 47, of Alexandria, Virginia, the former pastor of Crossroads Baptist Church in Bailey's Crossroads, Virginia, and a well-known figure in the Independent Fundamental Baptist movement, faces 13 criminal charges: two felony counts of unlawful contact with a minor, one felony count of corruption of minors, one misdemeanor count of corruption of minors, three misdemeanor counts of indecent exposure, and six misdemeanor counts of open lewdness.

According to the Pennsylvania State Police, Baldwin allegedly sent sexually explicit images of himself to a York County boy he met in November 2024, when he preached at the boy's church in Warrington Township and exchanged phone numbers with him. The boy was 15 years old when the contact began. Police say the images were sent multiple times a week over a period stretching from late 2024 to December 2025. Baldwin was arrested and arraigned on the charges on July 22, 2026. State police say they have reason to believe Baldwin may have shared explicit images with other juveniles, and anyone with information, or who may be a victim, is asked to contact the Pennsylvania State Police, York Station, at 717-428-1011.

After his arrest, Baldwin was removed from all ministry responsibilities at Crossroads Baptist Church, and a number of other ministries and institutions severed ties with him.

On October 7, 2026, Baldwin waived his preliminary hearing before Magisterial District Judge Jason Loper in District Court 19-3-10, and all 13 charges were sent up to the Court of Common Pleas. The docket shows the case papers were received by the Common Pleas court that same day.

Kenneth Baldwin is presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.
