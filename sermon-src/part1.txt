<h2>Let the Babies Come</h2>
<p>Glory be to God, everybody, in the highest. Ladies and gentlemen, brothers and sisters in Christ Jesus — family, friends, and foes. And yes, even foes in the family.</p>
<p>However, I do thank God for my quiver full of children. And I would encourage all of you young people to get married — to the opposite sex. Have all of the sex and fun you want to have, and let the babies come. Do not block the babies from coming. I know that sounds strange in America, the abortion factory of the world. Do not kill the children — pardon me, God forbid. Let the children come, and I assure you, God will bless you — particularly if you raise them right.</p>
<p>Will they be perfect? No. Tragically, they could be just as evil as you. You don't want that. Always remember that you reap more than you sow. "Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap." When you plant one apple seed, you're not going to get just one apple. You will get hundreds of apples a year — every year for the rest of your life.</p>
<p>But if you raise your children right, for the glory of God, based upon and rooted in the Word of God, you will be blessed. And you will also be blessed with precious memories as an extra blessing. I never thought about precious memories. I heard the song "Precious Memories," but it didn't mean anything to me until I raised my children and we served the Lord together. And every day, God floods my mind and my heart with precious memories. If God is involved in your life and in the raising of your children, buddy, you will enjoy some of the greatest videos from Heaven right in your own mind. What God does with precious memories, ladies and gentlemen, will blow your mind.</p>
<h2>The Song the Churches Stopped Singing</h2>
<p>Turn in your Bibles. But before I get into the Word of God, let me share some lyrics with you — an old familiar song that most of us have sung in the church for many years. I believe that many churches have stopped singing this song because they wanted to stop lying. It's called "I Surrender All."</p>
<p>I can't sing it, but I can read it real good. And that's where I live — I live with the words:</p>
<blockquote><p>All to Jesus I surrender,<br>All to Him I freely give;<br>I will ever love and trust Him,<br>In His presence daily live.<br>I surrender all, I surrender all,<br>All to Thee, my blessed Savior,<br>I surrender all.<br><br>All to Jesus I surrender,<br>Make me, Savior, wholly Thine;<br>Let me feel Thy Holy Spirit,<br>Truly know that Thou art mine.<br><br>All to Jesus I surrender,<br>Lord, I give myself to Thee;<br>Fill me with Thy love and power,<br>Let Thy blessing fall on me.</p></blockquote>
<p>Our title for this message today is: "I Surrender All" — Sex Is Important in Christian Marriage — The Five-Fold New Evangelical Devotional Order, Part 7, with yours truly, Daniel Whyte III, president of Gospel Light Society International and pastor of Gospel Light House of Prayer International.</p>
<h2>When Inertia Sets In</h2>
<p>Now, I want to preach in your hearing the New Evangelical Morning Devotional Order. Number one, from now on. You do as you please. And I hope that you've got some spontaneous sex going on. But the truth of the matter is, in most marriages, a lot of that spontaneity goes bye-bye. It's all a part of our sinful nature. It's all a part of the Fall. And lying about it is not going to help you.</p>
<p>Yes, after a while, by and by, even inertia — sex inertia — can grab hold of a couple. And when inertia comes along, it's like prayer inertia. A lot of people like to get ready to pray, but they don't pray. Many preachers will preach on prayer, but they don't pray themselves. Don't tell me they do, because they told me they don't.</p>
<p>When you first get saved, you're on that honeymoon with Jesus. Prayer is easy. Exciting. Reading the Bible is easy. Exciting. Same thing with sex. It's exciting — all the time, anywhere, everywhere. Then after a while, by and by, inertia sets in. Those of you who don't know what inertia is: it is that thing that you fight to do what you know you ought to do. Exercise inertia is another thing.</p>
<p>Many people in the world put exercise first. But the way the Lord has led me is to put sex first, then prayer and reading the Bible, and then get your exercise in, and then take a shower — in that order. Close to that order. When you throw the exercise in, which might not be but three days a week, then you have to turn it around a little bit.</p>
<h2>The Five-Fold New Evangelical Devotional Order</h2>
<p>But that devotional — that new devotional order — is this:</p>
<h3>Number One: Sex</h3>
<p>Number one, sex. Let's do the boom-boom together, as you grow in your marriage and your family life. And one of the benefits is, you can have sex — crazy and wild sex — because the children are still asleep, if you have children, and you know they don't want to wake up.</p>
<p>And you're not going to be set right in your relationship if you're not having sex on a regular basis. This idea of having sex once a month, once every two months, every other week, and all of that — I don't know where you got that from, because you didn't get it from the Bible. You think God gave you the blessing of marriage, and all you had to deal with in living with another sinful human being, to have sex once every month — every now and then on a special occasion?</p>
<p>And some of you men have bought into the sweet evangelical lie that the wife ought to lead in everything. And I'm here to tell you: if you let your wife lead in the sex area, buddy, you're going to be sexless. And you're going to be in a whole lot of trouble. And that's why you're always going to the porn factory, where God does not want you to go.</p>
<h3>Number Two: A Shower — Exercise, and Take a Shower</h3>
<p>Number two, take a shower — or exercise and take a shower. I couldn't find a word, an "S" word, for exercise. But exercise, then take a shower.</p>
<h3>Number Three: Supplication</h3>
<p>Three, supplication. Prayer.</p>
<h3>Number Four: Scripture</h3>
<p>Four, Scripture. Read the Bible.</p>
<h3>Number Five: Success</h3>
<p>Five, success — success for the rest of the day with the help of God. And leaving the house satisfied, if you have to leave the house, and ready for success in overcoming temptation — particularly sexual temptation. Sex is good. Very good.</p>
