<p>For those of you who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ as your Savior and prayed that prayer with me: may God bless you real good. Congratulations. Next steps: get baptized in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost — I don't care if they baptize you in a bathtub. "See, here is water; what doth hinder me to be baptized?" Start praying — you can start that immediately. Read the Bible; start in the Gospel of John, then read the entire New Testament, and then the entire Old Testament. Obey the Bible — Jesus Christ said, "If ye love me, keep my commandments." And witness to somebody else. Don't worry about anything. Pray about everything.</p>
<p>God loves you. Jesus Christ loves you. And I love you in the Lord, because of God's love shed abroad in my heart. Pray for me, as I pray for thee. Be a witness for the Lord.</p>
