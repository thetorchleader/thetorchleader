The Gospel — First, Every Day

How You Can Know for Sure You Are Going to Heaven

The Bible says all have sinned and come short of the glory of God (Romans 3:23), and the wages of sin is death — but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord (Romans 6:23). Jesus Christ, the Son of God, died on the cross for our sins, was buried, and rose again the third day (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life” (John 3:16).

If you will repent of your sins and put your faith in Jesus Christ alone, He will save you today. “That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved” (Romans 10:9). Pray and ask Him now — and then tell somebody.

About the Whyte House Christian Report
The Whyte House Christian Report is one of Pastor Daniel Whyte III’s earliest online publications — first published as the Whyte House Report, reporting Christian news since 2009, alongside its television companion, Whyte House TV. In its first season, the Report carried some 5,000 stories, gathered and published with the help of his daughter Danyelle-Elizabeth Whyte, who helped edit and publish the publication in those early years — part of the work Pastor Whyte’s children did to bring this ministry into the digital age, preaching the Gospel to thousands of people freely around the world. After a season of rest, the Report and the TV side now return together, as one publication: Christian family news, family devotions, and the news of evangelism and the persecuted church around the world — with the Gospel first, every day.
