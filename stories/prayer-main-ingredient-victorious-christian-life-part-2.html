Pastor Daniel Whyte III preached this message during the Sunday morning worship service at Gospel Light House of Prayer International on Sunday, November 22, 2026. The text is Luke 18:1-5. The full message runs about 33 minutes. Listen to the sermon above, and read the full transcript below.

Sermon Transcript: Prayer: The Main Ingredient for a Victorious Christian Life (Part 2) by Daniel Whyte III

"Ask, and ye shall receive; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened." I believe that. I have believed that for going on 50 years, and I believe that now.

Mr. Bobby Benjamin and Vivian Moore are asking me a question — with respect, no disrespect — "What can $3,000 do for you this weekend? Be honest." And I told Bobby Benjamin it would help our church, the worldwide outreach church here at Gospel Light House of Prayer International, tremendously, to reach more people with the Gospel in multiple ways. It would mean new equipment: a new lapel mic, a new mic, a new computer that helps us to get the Word out. And so, if there's anybody here — and I'm only doing this now because Sister Bliss, who was here in the wee hours of the morning, has already given generously to God's work this morning; I noticed that coming in — but I'm only saying this because Mr. Bobby Benjamin asked that question. And who knows? You never know what people have in their heart to do.

And so if anybody else wants to give, as did Sister Bliss this morning, as did Deacon Atkins and others, the best way to give directly is at Cash App, $DanielwhyteIII — that's W-H-Y-T-E, capital I, capital I, capital I. You can give whatever the Lord leads you to give. Just understand: no matter what you give, it is not going to cause me to compromise the Word of God. I'm unbought and unbossed. I'm not influenced by money. I'm not here for money. God has taken care of me and my family by faith for over 47 years now. And so, ladies and gentlemen, brothers and sisters in Christ Jesus, family, friends, and foes — and yes, foes in the family even — I thank God for my quiver full of children who have come out of the loins of my body.

And I praise the Lord for each and every one of them, for when you see me live on multiple channels, my children are a big part of that, for they have brought us into the digital age. As little children, they did it. And so, ladies and gentlemen, brothers and sisters in Christ Jesus, we would like to say thank you for joining us. Bear with us, everybody. But you see how God could use you, just based upon what happened here just a minute ago. JT, so glad to have you. Glory be to God in the highest.

Turn in your Bibles this morning, ladies and gentlemen, to Luke chapter 18. If you want to have a consistently successful life, I want to continue to preach in your hearing: "Prayer: The Main Ingredient for a Victorious Christian Life." If you want to live a victorious Christian life, you must stay in connection with God Almighty in and through prayer. Prayer: the main ingredient for a victorious Christian life. Luke chapter 18, verses 1 through 5: "And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint."

No matter what is going on, just keep on praying. He said, "Preacher, I have trouble in my marriage." Keep on praying, and never quit. Don't faint. Don't stop. Don't get weary in well-doing. "And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint." Craig Stallings, Lydia, Tyrone Jenkins, The Mirror 369, Nickom 63, D Grinds the rapper, Jenny Rose — based upon that verse in the Holy Bible: "And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint."

Holy Father God, I pray in the holy name of the Lord Jesus Christ. Hallowed be Thy name. Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. And Holy Father God, based upon Your holy Word that we have already read three times, Lord, we pray in faith, believing. We praise You, Lord, and thank You for Brother Mark being with us, even though he has his own church; he stops by every now and then. And we pray for salvation and spiritual, family and life, financial and material protection and provision, mental and physical blessings, Lord, upon him and his mother and his immediate family, his wife and his children. And Lord, we pray that You will rebuke and bind the devil, his demons, and his hosts from each and every one of us: Brother Mark and his family, Sister Bliss and her family, Deacon Moab and his family, Deacon Karamba and his family, Sister Gypsy Heart and her family, and everybody else on this beautiful Sunday morning. Give us, Lord God in heaven, sweet victory over the world, the flesh, and the devil. Help us all to be prayerful, sober-minded, vigilant, and watchful throughout this morning and throughout this day.

Even though, Lord, it looks like this day will be paved for us by You, help us not to take You for granted. Help us not to take things for granted. Help us to enjoy the day, as we thank You for blessing us to enjoy so many days down through the years. But Lord, at the same time, help us to be sober-minded, vigilant, prayerful, and watchful, for the devil is seeking to destroy us all, to block us and to hinder us. For Jesus Christ's sake, Holy Father God, have mercy and grace upon me and upon all of my children, all of my quiver full, and Lord, all people who are born again and saved, and who are believers in Jesus Christ. Where we have sinned in word, thought, and deed, for Jesus Christ's sake, please forgive us, Lord, of our sins, our faults, and our failures. Wash and cleanse our hearts, our minds, our souls, our spirits, and our consciences in the holy and precious blood of Christ. Make us, Lord, to be whiter than snow on the inside.

Deliver us, Lord, this morning from temptation, evil, and sin. Grant us, Lord, Your grace and Your strength and the power of Your Holy Spirit to love right, to live right, to think right, to do right, and to act right, and to do that which is pleasing in Your sight. Revive us again. Restore us again. Renew us again. Truly lead us, guide us, and direct us throughout this morning, this afternoon, and this evening, and throughout this night, in the way that You would have us to go. Order our steps and direct our ways.

And Holy Father God, I pray in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, for those of us who are believers in You, Lord Jesus Christ, for those of us who are born again, saved by Your grace: help us, Lord, to humble ourselves, to pray, to seek Your face, and to turn from our wicked ways, and to repent of our wicked sins. And Holy Father God, help us to change and be more like You. Grant us Your grace and the power of Your Holy Spirit to love right, to live right, to think right, to do right, to act right, and to do that which is pleasing in Your sight. And Holy Father God, Lord, help us to humble ourselves, to pray, to seek Your face, and to turn from our wicked ways, and to repent of our sins. And Holy Father God, I pray in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ for, and help us to get back to You, our first love.

And Holy Father God, as You know, my wife of over 40 years — I've been praying for her that long — is full of hell and the devil and sickness because of her sins, her pride, her stubbornness, her rebelliousness, and her foolishness, and she's blinded to it, as I was. I recognize where she is and what's going on in her life, because I used to be that way myself: religious, churchgoing, but lost. And the Christian life cannot be faked. So Lord, I pray again for Mrs. Whyte, my wife of nearly 40 years, and Lord, You will open her blinded eyes and stop her deaf ears and help her to see the light. And I pray that she will pray the prayer of salvation once again, for her own good, even right now. And Holy Father God, even as I pray, Lord, I pray that she will believe in You, that others will believe in You, as I share with people the bad news side of the Gospel and the good news side of the Gospel right now, from the jump, in this service, in case we don't make it to the end of this service. Thank you — Rodade Andrews, Sigma, Nina, Brother Mark 110, Ricky, Kelly, Matthew, Ruth — I pray for salvation and spiritual, family and life, financial and material protection and provision blessings upon each and every one of you.

But right now, I want you to understand the bad news side of the Gospel and the good news side of the Gospel. I am impressed of the Lord to deal with that first this morning. Please understand with me: bad news number one is that you and yours truly — we are all wicked and evil sinners. "Preacher, are you calling me a wicked and evil sinner?" No, God called you a wicked and evil sinner. We have wickedness and evil in us, and nobody is perfect. Nobody is holy as we should be. As I said over the past few weeks, there is a wicked, demonic love affair with not only sin, but evil. And I want you to understand that we are all downright evil in God's sight. Yet God loves us. God shows us mercy. For the Word of God says, from the lips of Jesus Christ: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life." God sent His Son. We're in such bad shape as sinners, God had to send His Son, Jesus Christ, to sacrifice His life for our sins, so that we will have an opportunity and the privilege of escaping our punishment — our just punishment, which is the burning hell — so that we will one day be able to go to heaven, by believing in Jesus Christ.

For the Bible says — bad news number two — "the wages of sin is death." The wages of sin is death. We die because of our wicked, evil, and ungodly sins. Lying — they say, "What sins, preacher?" Lying. Have you ever told a lie? Some people lie so much, they believe the lies themselves. Stealing: you have a thief in your family; things just keep coming up missing, and there's only two of you in the household, so you know who's stealing, and then they'll lie about it. And the bad news about that is that God said that all liars shall have their part in the lake of fire.

Bad news number three: you will die. But not only will you die, you will face judgment before God Almighty at the Great White Throne judgment. You will die and go to hell, and you will die and go eventually to the lake of fire. For God's Word says: all liars, all whoremongers, all whores, people who practice sexual sin outside of the bonds of marriage of any kind, including homosexuality — the Bible says they will have their part in the lake of fire. Your unbelief about that does not change the fact, because it is true through and through.

So here's the good news. As bad as you want to be, as evil as you are — not only are we sinners, we're evil, always plotting something evil, always plotting something bad; can't rest until we're doing evil — the good news is, Jesus Christ said the most important words in the history of the world when He said: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."

Max from the Ukraine — and I've been praying for you; in fact, we prayed for you all yesterday — so glad to have you, all the way from the Ukraine, on TikTok. Avery, so glad to have you. Lin, so glad to have you. Kyuya. And so, ladies and gentlemen, believe in your heart in the Son of God, the Lord Jesus Christ. Pray and ask Him to save your soul from the burning hell, from the torture of your sins, and then the torture and punishment of your sins in the burning hell. Follow me in the sinner's prayer. Believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for you — He suffered, bled, and died on the cross for you, for your sins, was buried, and rose on the third day. With over 2,400 with us right now, pray and ask Jesus Christ to come into your heart and save your soul. Be sincere about it. Do not pray it if you don't mean it. Don't act like you believe it if you don't. God does not make people get saved; He gives you the opportunity to get saved, by believing in His Son, the Lord Jesus Christ. This is based upon your free will. Follow me in the sinner's prayer, and mean it from your heart:

"Holy Father God, have mercy and grace upon me, a sinner. For Jesus Christ's sake, please forgive me of all of my evil, ugly sins, my failures and my faults, as I now believe in You, Lord Jesus Christ. I believe with all of my heart that You suffered, You bled, and You died on the cross for my sins, was buried, and rose on the third day. Lord Jesus Christ, please come into my heart and into my spirit. Please save my soul from my sins, from Satan, from the devil and the demons of hell, and from hell itself. Fill me with Your Holy Spirit. Help me to repent of my evil ways. Help me to turn from my evil ways and change, for Your glory, Your praise, and Your honor. And help me to follow You in the newness of life. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, and for His sake. Amen."

Holy Father God, I pray now for all of these people who have believed in You by faith. And Lord, I say congratulations to them for doing the most important thing in life, and that is believing in You, praying to You, and asking You to save their souls from the burning hell. And now, Lord, I pray that You'll help them to get baptized. Help them, Lord, to follow You in believer's baptism, in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost. And then, Holy Father God, I pray that You will help them to start praying every day, asking You for help in the name of Jesus Christ, based upon Your holy Word: "And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint." And so, ladies and gentlemen, as we come to a close, a brief review. I want to share with you a message titled "Prayer: The Main Ingredient for a Victorious Christian Life."
